# Provenance

This repository combines maintained source from PRAXIS-VirtualCell, PRAXIS-MDR-GNB and the direct-MIC portion of the A. baumannii world-model project. Original source trees, delivery bundles and uncommitted historical changes are retained in `../PRAXIS-virtualcell-old/`.

Local MDR artifacts preserve their original catalog and serialized class names. AB recovered weights belong to the 2026-09-13 delivery; they do not replace the later 2026-09-23 benchmark identities. See [consolidation](docs/CONSOLIDATION.md), [asset inventory](docs/DATA-ASSETS.md) and [data licenses](docs/DATA-LICENSES.md).

The pre-consolidation VirtualCell source was based on commit `8bb7195852368306f3041d0082bc6fdf96385b7f`; the MDR source was based on `37c7c39ebaa3563b392cd7f473b5aaa8122daa11` with local modifications. The archive contains the full MDR working tree and the recorded pre-migration tracked patches/status. These commit identities do not imply a clean original working tree.
