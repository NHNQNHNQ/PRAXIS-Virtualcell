01 / Architecture

Compose the experiment.

Scientific components connect through explicit biological contracts in the standalone VirtualCell runtime.

VirtualCell agents support reasoning and communication.

Question interpretation, candidate routing, and result explanation are tied to structured experiment records.

The runtime controls scientific execution.

Compatibility, predictor outputs, validation evidence, and the final execution verdict remain under runtime authority.

02 / Workflow

Five stages. One auditable experiment.

Select a stage to follow an illustrative research question from interpretation to its final record.

03 / Biological systems

One runtime. Native biology.

Different organisms retain their own features, perturbations, models, and readouts within the VirtualCell scientific runtime.

01 / BACTERIA

Escherichia coli

GROWTH

Gene knockout → growth

Mechanistic metabolic models connect genetic interventions and growth conditions to a defined phenotype.

Representative model: iML1515
02 / YEAST

S. cerevisiae

FITNESS

Gene deletion → fitness

Task-specific contracts preserve deletion identity, culture conditions, and the semantics of fitness readouts.

Representative model: iMM904
03 / HUMAN CELLS

Human K562 cells

TRANSCRIPTION

Perturbation → expression

Data-driven models support high-dimensional responses and condition-level comparison under fixed feature contracts.

Examples: additive models · GEARS

04 / Evidence

Evidence belongs in the experiment.

Execution identity and biological support travel with each result. Evidence categories retain distinct meanings.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

Development validation

Performance on a defined development or Golden benchmark, interpreted within that protocol's scope.

02 / EXTERNAL CONTEXT

External stress testing

Behavior on independent sources, changed conditions, or distribution shifts, with the relevant limitations preserved.

03 / COMPUTATION

Technical reproducibility

Consistent results under fixed inputs, models, adapters, environments, and execution configurations.

04 / BIOLOGICAL SUPPORT

Scientific validation

An independent evidence state. Successful execution or replay alone does not establish biological validity.

Included

K562 protocols, evaluation scripts, and recorded research results.

External assets

Large datasets, fitted artifacts, and trained model checkpoints.

Packaging scope

Complete real-data microbial benchmark pipelines are not bundled.
