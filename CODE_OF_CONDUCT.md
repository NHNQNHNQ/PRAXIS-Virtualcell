# Code of Conduct

We collaborate with respect, scientific honesty and care for user and research data.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, clear attribution, reproducible claims,
respect for privacy and consent, and prompt correction of overstated scientific or product status. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, secret disclosure, deliberate provenance removal and fabrication of evidence
or promotion state are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers
will limit disclosure, avoid conflicts of interest and apply proportionate actions, including warnings,
temporary restrictions or removal from project spaces. Good-faith reporting and scientific dissent must not be
retaliated against.
